Hi, I’m Jessica.

I work with organisations on architecture and engineering leadership: turning complex platforms into something the business can grow with, and leaving the teams around those systems in a better place than I found them.

That might mean covering a senior leadership role for a defined period, or helping you see a platform clearly enough to decide what to do next. The goal is the same either way: to leave things in a better state than I found them, both the code and the culture.

How I work

Technical debt is not just a code problem. It is a people problem: the teams who maintain the systems, the stakeholders who rely on them, and the outcomes the business actually needs. I would rather a solution your engineers can own than a clever design that only works while I am in the room.

  • With the team, not instead of it. I work alongside your engineers and architects, so they understand the why as well as the what, and can keep improving things after the engagement ends.
  • Strategy and the detail. I am comfortable in both: the board-level conversation, and the architecture of a messy legacy system. Proposals stay practical. They have to survive contact with production, audits, and the people who will run them.
  • Clear communication. Stakeholders get a straight account of risk, options, and trade-offs. I would rather name the constraint than dress up a weak plan.
  • Change that lasts. The work should move the business (resilience, delivery, regulation) without burning out the people who support the system.

Experience

Most of my career has been architecture and engineering leadership in highly regulated environments, including as Head of Architecture. The work has covered large platforms and messy legacy systems, and the organisational side of technical change: how teams are set up, how decisions get made, and how strategy survives contact with delivery.

Typical problems have been aligning a platform with business goals; making compliance something engineering can actually operate, including PCI and data governance; technical roadmaps that can compete fairly with product work; and the architecture of systems that have grown through years of local decisions.

That includes covering senior roles (CTO, Head of Engineering, Head of Architecture) and advisory work on systems strategy, assessments, and targeted architectural support.

Recent examples are on the Case Studies page. The current offer is on Services.

Community

I have been involved in the tech community for a long time, including a term on the .NET Foundation Board Of Directors, co-founding DDD East Midlands Conference, and speaking at conferences internationally. Topics have included stabilising legacy systems, monitoring culture, and architecture.
