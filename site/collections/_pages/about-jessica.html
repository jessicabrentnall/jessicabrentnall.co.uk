Hi, I’m Jessica.

I am an architecture and engineering leader. The work I take on is Head of Architecture, CTO, and Head of Engineering: covering the role for a defined period, or helping you see a platform clearly enough to decide what to do next.

The goal is the same either way: to leave things in a better state than I found them, both the code and the culture.

How I work

Technical debt is not just a code problem. It is a people problem: the teams who maintain the systems, the stakeholders who rely on them, and the outcomes the business actually needs. I would rather a solution your engineers can own than a clever design that only works while I am in the room.

  • With the team, not instead of it. I work alongside your engineers and architects, so they understand the why as well as the what, and can keep improving things after the engagement ends.
  • Strategy and the detail. I am comfortable in both: the board-level conversation, and the architecture of a messy legacy system. Proposals stay practical. They have to survive contact with production, audits, and the people who will run them.
  • Clear communication. Stakeholders get a straight account of risk, options, and trade-offs. I would rather name the constraint than dress up a weak plan.
  • Change that lasts. The work should move the business (resilience, delivery, regulation) without burning out the people who support the system.

Experience

Most of my career has been architecture and engineering leadership in highly regulated environments, in companies from eight-person startups through to large organisations. I have been Head of Architecture at NewDay, and I have taken interim CTO work, including the technology and engineering plan for a business-critical initiative.

At NewDay I was the technical interface for an 18-month client onboarding programme across 52 engineering teams, translating contractual obligations into architectural roadmaps with security, data, platform, and engineering leadership. I built and scaled the architecture function, moved design into the teams so it was not a bottleneck, and put architecture roadmaps into the product lifecycle with ExCo reporting on progress, cost, and risk. Compliance has included PCI DSS v4 and ISO 27001.

Before that I was a technical architect at the Legal Aid Agency, including the migration of a 30-year-old estate onto modern services on AWS, and the guilds (observability, testing, security) that make that kind of change stick. Typical problems have been aligning a platform with business and regulatory goals; making PCI and data governance something engineering can operate; technical roadmaps that can compete fairly with product work; and systems that have grown through years of local decisions.

That includes covering senior roles (CTO, Head of Engineering, Head of Architecture) and advisory work on systems strategy, assessments, and targeted architectural support. Recent examples are on the Case Studies page. The current offer is on Services.

Community

I have been involved in the tech community for a long time, including a term on the .NET Foundation Board Of Directors, co-founding DDD East Midlands Conference, and speaking at conferences internationally. Topics have included stabilising legacy systems, monitoring culture, and architecture.
